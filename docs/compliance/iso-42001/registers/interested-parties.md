## Context (clause 4.1)

### External issues

- **Regulatory.** EU AI Act phased application; growing US state-level
  AI laws (e.g., Colorado AI Act, NYC AEDT); sectoral AI guidance.
- **Technology.** Rapid foundation-model release cadence; new
  modalities (voice, vision); growth of agent / tool-use patterns; MCP
  adoption.
- **Market.** Clients increasingly require AI governance attestation
  (SOC 2 + ISO 27001 → ISO 42001).
- **Reputational.** Open-source repo visibility creates downside if a
  demo behaves unsafely.

### Internal issues

- Small team; no in-house ML training infrastructure.
- Heavy dependence on third-party foundation-model APIs.
- Mix of demo / educational and (future) client production work.
- Limited budget for tooling — AIMS must be lightweight.
