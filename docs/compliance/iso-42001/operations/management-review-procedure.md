# Management Review Procedure

```
Owner:        [TBD - AIMS Lead]
Approved by:  [TBD - Managing Member]
Effective:    [TBD]
Last review:  [TBD]
Next review:  [TBD - annual]
Coverage:     Clause 9.3 · 10.2
```

## 1. Purpose

The Managing Member of 12th House AI, LLC reviews the AIMS at planned
intervals to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and
effectiveness.

## 2. Frequency

At least **annually**. An additional review is triggered by:

- A High or Critical incident.
- A material change in scope, law, or AIMS structure.
- A major audit finding.

## 3. Inputs (clause 9.3.2)

The AIMS Lead prepares a pack covering, at minimum:

1. **Status of actions** from the previous management review.
2. **Changes** in external & internal issues relevant to the AIMS.
3. **Performance of the AIMS:**
   - Achievement of AI objectives from
     [`../policies/ai-policy.md`](../policies/ai-policy.md) § 4.
   - KPIs and monitoring results (clause 9.1).
   - Nonconformities and corrective actions (NC log).
   - Audit results (internal + any external).
   - Incident summary.
4. **AI risk assessment results** —
   [`../registers/risk-register.md`](../registers/risk-register.md)
   trend.
5. **AI impact assessment results** — summary of recent AIIAs.
6. **Feedback** from interested parties (clients, learners, staff,
   public).
7. **Status of risks** and effectiveness of treatment.
8. **Opportunities** for continual improvement.
9. **Changes** to the AIMS that may be needed.

## 4. Outputs (clause 9.3.3)

Decisions and actions on:

- Continual improvement opportunities.
- Need for changes to the AIMS (including scope, policy, controls).
- Resource needs.
- Changes to AI objectives.

Each output is captured as an action item with **owner**, **due date**,
and **acceptance criterion**.

## 5. Records

- Agenda
- Pre-read pack (inputs above)
- Attendance
- Minutes & decisions
- Action register update

Stored at `registers/management-review/MR-YYYY-NN.md`. Retained for at
least 3 years.

## 6. Quorum

- Managing Member: **required**.
- AIMS Lead: **required**.
- Engineering & advisor: invited; not required for quorum.

## 7. Worked agenda template

```
1. Welcome & purpose
2. Review of prior actions
3. Context: external & internal changes
4. AIMS performance:
   - Objectives
   - KPIs
   - Audit findings
   - NCs & corrective actions
   - Incidents
5. Risk & impact assessment summary
6. Interested-party feedback
7. Opportunities for improvement
8. Decisions:
   - Scope changes
   - Policy changes
   - Resource changes
   - Updated AI objectives
9. Action register update
10. Close
```
