## Competence (clause 7.2)

For each AIMS role, define the competence baseline and how it is
maintained.

| Role | Required competence | Evidence | Refresh cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Managing Member | AIMS overview, executive AI risk awareness | Attendance at AIMS overview session | Annual |
| AIMS Lead | ISO/IEC 42001 working knowledge; ideally Lead Auditor or Lead Implementer cert | PECB / equivalent certificate | 3 years (recert) |
| Engineering | Secure & responsible AI development practices; familiarity with the LLC's SDLC procedure | Onboarding doc sign-off + annual refresh | Annual |
| External advisor | Legal/regulatory background (AI Act, US state AI laws, sectoral) | Engagement letter | Per engagement |

### Competence records

Stored at `registers/competence/<name>.md` (created on first hire).
Each record lists training completed, certifications, and review
dates. Records are retained for the duration of the role plus 3 years.

## Awareness (clause 7.3)

All staff and contractors complete an awareness session covering:

1. The LLC's AI policy and where to find it.
2. Why the AIMS exists (business + regulatory context).
3. Their personal responsibilities under the AUP.
4. How to report concerns and incidents.
5. The lifecycle stages and where AIIAs and system cards fit.

Awareness is delivered:

- **At onboarding**, before access to any in-scope system.
- **Annually**, as a short refresh.
- **Ad hoc**, after material policy changes or any High/Critical
  incident.

### Awareness records

`registers/awareness/YYYY-<topic>.md` — list of attendees, date,
content covered, acknowledgment.

## Auditor's exam-prep note

For Lead Auditor study, this document is the connection point between
the AIMS and "people". Auditors typically test:

- Whether competence has been **determined** (not just assumed).
- Whether evidence of competence is **on file** and current.
- Whether awareness reaches **all relevant persons**, including
  contractors.
