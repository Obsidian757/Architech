## 1. Purpose

Define responsible use of AI systems by **12th House AI, LLC** staff and
by users of LLC-provided AI systems (including Architech demos).

## 2. Scope

- LLC staff and contractors using any AI system in the course of LLC work.
- Users of LLC-developed AI systems and Architech demo apps.

## 3. Intended use

Each in-scope system declares its **intended use** and **foreseeable
non-intended use** in its system card
([`../templates/model-system-card.md`](../templates/model-system-card.md)
§ "Intended use / Out of scope use"). Operating a system outside its
intended use is prohibited without a new AIIA.

## 4. Prohibited uses

The following are prohibited for any AI system in scope:

1. Generating illegal content (CSAM, weapon-design instructions, etc.).
2. Generating content that defames identifiable individuals.
3. Making **consequential automated decisions** about individuals —
   employment, credit, housing, legal status, healthcare — without
   documented human review.
4. Surveillance of individuals without lawful basis.
5. Disclosing confidential client or LLC data to a third-party model
   provider unless covered by a signed DPA or equivalent.
6. Use that violates the model provider's terms of service.

## 5. Required practices

When using AI systems in LLC work, staff:

1. **Disclose AI use** when AI materially contributes to client-facing
   output.
2. **Review outputs before delivery** — never ship raw model output as
   the final artifact in client work.
3. **Protect inputs** — do not paste client confidential data into
   external models unless the engagement allows it.
4. **Report concerns** via the channel in
   [`ai-policy.md`](ai-policy.md) § 8.
5. **Log incidents** affecting users of LLC systems.

## 6. Monitoring (A.9 + clause 8)

For each operated system, the system card defines what use-time
monitoring exists (rate, content, drift). Demo apps may have **no
monitoring**; that decision is recorded in the system card.

## 7. Enforcement

Violations are handled by the AIMS Lead. Sanctions range from required
retraining to termination of the engagement.

## 8. Review

Annual; or after any High/Critical incident.
